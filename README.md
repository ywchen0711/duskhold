# Duskhold 暗月迷城

An action RPG in the spirit of the classic dungeon crawlers, drawn in the Breezy Isle look (cel shading, outlines, a fine pixel pass). All names and content are original.

深入地下城、打倒三大魔王、收集傳說裝備的動作角色扮演遊戲。

Play: https://ywchen0711.github.io/duskhold/

- **Classes:** Warrior, Ranger and Sorceress. Each has six skills up to level 20 and two passives or buffs, with stat points and skill points on every level-up.
- **Acts:** three acts (the Catacombs, the Sunken Temple, the Ember Abyss). Each has three randomly built levels and a boss lair.
- **Monsters:** packs, champion packs and named unique monsters with modifiers. Each boss has its own special attacks.
- **Loot:** normal, magic, rare and unique items, with 22 affixes, 34 bases and 18 uniques; magic find; a gambler. Also gold, potions, urns and chests.
- **Camp:** a merchant, a healer, a gambler, a stash, waypoints and town portal scrolls.
- **Difficulty:** Normal, Nightmare and Hell (resistance penalties, stronger monsters, better drops).
- **Saves:** several characters per browser, mirrored to the site's cloud save.
- **Tests:** `node tools/test.mjs` checks level connectivity, loot distribution and magic find, and fight balance per class and act.
